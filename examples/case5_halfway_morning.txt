# Case 5 from docs/spec/test-cases.md: the opening price is a price nobody quoted.
# Rules 1 and 2 tie between 100.00 and 101.00, and the previous close sits exactly halfway between them.
PREV_CLOSE 100.50

PHASE PREOPEN
ADD 11 SELL LIMIT 100.00 100
ADD 1 BUY LIMIT 101.00 100
PHASE LIMIT_ONLY
PHASE AUCTION                  # opens at 100.50: the previous close itself
PHASE CONTINUOUS
ADD 2 BUY LIMIT 100.40 50      # nobody is selling yet, so it waits
ADD 12 SELL MARKET 30          # takes 30 from buyer 2 at 100.40
